# Devices

| Device | Status |
|---|---|
| Supernote **Nomad** (A6 X2) | Tested end to end: uploads and downloads, capacity, conflicts, deletes, To-Do sync, a restore. |
| Other models: A5 X, A6 X, **Manta** (A5 X2), … | Not tested. They speak the same protocol and very likely work. |

## Have another model?

Trying it and reporting back is one of the most useful things a beta tester can
do.

1. [Copy the tablet's folders](@/manual/point-supernote.md) to another computer
   first.
2. Point it at Inkhold, sync a few notebooks both ways, and open them in the
   browser.
3. [Open an issue](https://github.com/m00qek/inkhold/issues) with the model and
   its firmware version, and what worked or didn't.
