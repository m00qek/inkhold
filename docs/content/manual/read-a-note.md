# Read a note

Open a notebook in the browser and read it page by page. Your browser draws
the pages, with the same ink as on the tablet.

- **Turn pages** with the arrows in the bar, the arrow keys, or a swipe on a
  phone. **Pages** shows every page, to jump to one.
- **Zoom** with **+** and **−**, or a pinch on a phone. **Fit** fits the page
  to the screen again.
- **Find in this note** (Ctrl+F) searches its recognised handwriting and typed
  text, and marks each match on the page. A match in a text box marks the whole
  box: the tablet records where each recognised word is, but only where typed
  text's box is.
- **Recognized text** shows the text the tablet recognised on the page.
- **Layers** shows a page's layers, each on its own, to hide the ones you don't
  want to see. It is available when the page has more than one.

![A notebook open in the browser: the page on the right, the Pages panel listing every page on the left, and the bar with paging, zoom, Find and the panels.](screenshots/reader.png)

A sketch (`.spd`) opens the same way, with its layers' opacity too. Text files
and images open in their own viewers; PDFs have
[their own page](@/manual/pdf.md).
