# Use To-Do in the browser

See your tablet's To-Do lists and tasks in the browser, and change them there:
the tablet picks the changes up at its next sync. To-Do sync must be
[turned on](@/manual/todo-sync.md) first.

Choose **To-Do** in the library's left rail. **Inbox**, **Planned**, **All** and
**Completed** sit under it; plain **To-Do** shows every list.

- **Add a task**: type in the box at the top and choose **Add**. The calendar
  beside it gives it a day.
- **Tick it off**: the circle in front of it.
- **Change it**: its **…** menu has **Rename**, **Move to…**, a day,
  **Move up**, **Move down** and **Delete**. Or drag it by its handle.
- **A task written on a notebook page** shows the handwriting it was written
  in: choose it to open that page.
- **Download** saves the list: **Tasks**, a calendar file (`.ics`) other apps
  import, or **Plain text**.

Until the tablet has synced a change you made, the page says it is waiting for
the tablet.

**Keyboard shortcuts**, under the list, shows the keys for all of this.
