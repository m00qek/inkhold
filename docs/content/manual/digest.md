# Read your digests in the browser

See the passages and handwriting you collected in the tablet's Digest app, go
from each back to the page it came from, and change what you typed about it.
Digest sync must be [turned on](@/manual/digest-sync.md) first. Everything else
stays the tablet's: you make, move and delete digests there, and write their
handwritten notes there; the page catches up after the tablet syncs.

Choose **Digest** in the library's left rail. Like the tablet's app, it has two
ways in:

- **Categories**: **All**, **Uncategorized** and the categories you made,
  newest digest first.
- **Sources**: the documents and notes your digests come from, and what you
  typed on the tablet. A document's digests are in page order, so they read
  like notes on it from top to bottom.

Choose a digest to open it beside the list: the whole passage, your comment,
the note you wrote on it, its category and when you made it.

- **Open at page** opens the document or notebook at that page. The reader's
  back button returns to the digest.
- **Copy text** copies the passage.
- **Edit**, beside **Comment**, changes the typed comment (**Add a comment**
  when there is none), up to 2000 characters, as on the tablet. **Save
  comment** keeps it; **Escape** or **Cancel** leaves it as it was. Until the
  tablet syncs, the digest says it is waiting for the tablet. If the tablet
  changed the same comment meanwhile, the later change wins.
- **Escape**, or the **×**, closes it.

A digest whose file is no longer in the library (it was never synced, or it
moved) can't be opened, and says so.
