# Find a note

Search everything at once: notebook and file names, your handwriting, the text
of PDFs and text files, and your to-dos.

1. Type in the search box at the top of the library. On a phone, choose
   **Search** in the bar at the bottom.
2. Results appear as you type, best first. Each says how many pages matched
   and quotes the line it found.
3. Open a result: it opens on the page the quoted line comes from, with the
   matches marked. **Find in this note** steps through the rest.

![Searching for “desert”: one result, the notebook Ozymandias, with the line it matched quoted under it and the matched word marked.](screenshots/search.png)

## What it finds

- **Handwriting**, from the text your Supernote recognised itself. A notebook
  the tablet never recognised can't be searched; the line under the results,
  for example "Text is searchable on 84 of 161 pages with writing",
  says how much is covered, and **Which notes** lists the ones that aren't.
- **Typed text**, from a notebook's text boxes, whether or not the tablet
  recognises the notebook.
- **PDFs and text files**, from their own text. Handwriting on a PDF is not
  searchable: see [What doesn't work yet](@/manual/limits.md).
- **Close spellings.** With no exact match, it says "No exact match — showing
  close matches.", so a typo still finds the word you meant.

Search runs in your browser: the first search after opening the library may
take a moment while it gathers what it needs.
